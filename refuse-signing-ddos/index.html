What the Coordinator Actually Does

  • A ban is a fixed amount of BTC-hours

    Ban length is DoSSeverity ÷ coin value × penalty factor × 1.3^(offenses − 1). Coin value × ban length therefore always comes to 0.1 BTC·h on a first refusal. A 10,000-sat coin sits out 1,000 hours; a 0.3 BTC coin sits out 20 minutes.

    Prison.cs, GetBanTimePeriod · WabiSabiConfig.cs: DoSSeverity = 0.1 BTC

  • Short bans are thrown away

    Any ban under DoSMinTimeInPrison (20 min) becomes zero. With the default severity, a coin above 0.3 BTC is not banned at all on its first refusal. A 1 BTC coin can refuse five times before any ban applies.

    Prison.cs, EffectiveMinTimeFrame

  • Spent coins start clean today

    Prison.InheritPunishment still exists, but nothing calls it. Descendant banning landed in #11364 (Aug 2023) and was halved per spend in #11940. The CoinJoinIdStore removal in #13228 (Jul 2024) dropped the ! from its filter, so from then on only coinjoin outputs inherited bans. The IoC rewiring in #13076 (Dec 2024) then removed the call entirely.

    git log -S "InheritPunishment(" · 923ba7f25f, 3621111fdd, 59b908c0b8, 00288b29d2

  • One refusal buys one blame round, not one full round

    After a failed signing, the coordinator starts a blame round for the coins that did sign. Honest clients follow blame rounds with no retry limit. The attacker's other coins in that round are whitelisted too, so the chain goes on. With default timeouts each blame round lasts at most 3 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 6 minutes. A chain ends when the attacker runs out of coins in it or the round drops below MinInputCountByBlameRound. A round holds at most MaxInputCountByRound inputs, so one round supports at most max − min + 1 refusals.

    Arena.cs, EndRoundAndTryCreateBlameRoundAsync · CoinJoinClient.cs "Keep going to blame round until there's none"

  • Mass refusals get a flat, short ban

    When more coins fail at once than the round's minimum input count, the coordinator treats it as its own fault. Each coin gets a stability ban of 20 minutes plus 0 to 3 hours, whatever its value. This calculator does not model that route.

    Arena.cs, ReasonableOffendersCount → Prison.CoordinatorStabilitySafetyBan

What the Model Simplifies

  • Refusals come at a steady pace: one every refusal cost ÷ rounds kept failing. Each coin sits in a round for one refusal cost before it refuses.
  • All coins are the same size, and respending doesn't shrink them. A note appears when laundering one ban costs more than the coin is worth.
  • “Respend banned coins” chains the respends in one block and waits for one confirmation. Batching many coins into one transaction brings the per-coin size down toward 100 vB.
  • With half inheritance, a descendant's ban keeps the original start time, as #11940 did. The wait-out route is the same in every inheritance mode, because it never spends a banned coin.
  • Cost of capital is capital × annual rate × attack length. It measures time value only, not the risk of holding the coins.
Source: rules and defaults read from WalletWasabi master (Prison.cs, Arena.cs, WabiSabiConfig.cs) in September 2026. A coordinator can change every one of these settings in its config.
